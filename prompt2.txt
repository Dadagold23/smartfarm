# MASTER SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT PROMPT
# AGROSMART IRRIGATION INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM (ASIIS)
### AI-Powered Smart Agriculture, Irrigation, Crop Protection, Harvest Intelligence and Farm Security Platform

## 1. PROJECT VISION

Act as a senior software architect, full-stack engineer, AI/ML engineer, IoT engineer, agricultural technology specialist, database architect and cybersecurity engineer.

Design and develop a complete, production-oriented, AI-powered agricultural management platform named:

**AgroSmart Irrigation Intelligence System (ASIIS).**

The platform must combine Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Internet of Things (IoT), agricultural research, environmental monitoring, intelligent irrigation, weed identification, chemical spraying, farm intrusion detection and crop maturity prediction.

The system must be capable of managing multiple farms, agricultural sections, crop varieties, irrigation zones, sprinkler devices, chemical application equipment, sensors and farm security infrastructure from a unified platform.

The objective is to develop an intelligent agricultural ecosystem that reduces manual intervention, improves water-use efficiency, supports crop protection, provides early security alerts and helps farmers plan harvesting activities.

## 3. COMPLETE 22-MODULE IMPLEMENTATION

### MODULE 1: Authentication and Access Management
Implement secure registration, login, logout, password recovery, email verification, two-factor authentication, role-based permissions and session management.

Roles must include Super Administrator, Farm Owner, Farm Manager, Agronomist, Agricultural Officer, Irrigation Technician, Security Officer, Field Supervisor and Data Analyst.

### MODULE 2: Farm and Agricultural Land Management
Implement multiple farm registration, GPS coordinates, land boundaries, geographical mapping, agricultural sections, irrigation zones, soil profiles and farm infrastructure management.

Support GIS-based farm visualisation.

### MODULE 3: AI Crop and Plant Identification
Implement image-based plant identification using computer vision.

Identify crop species, varieties where supported, growth stages and visible plant abnormalities.

Provide confidence scores, alternative classifications and manual verification.

### MODULE 4: Agricultural Knowledge Database
Create a comprehensive agricultural knowledge repository containing crop characteristics, growth stages, soil requirements, crop coefficients, root depths, maturity durations, irrigation requirements, weed information and approved agricultural practices.

Use verified research from FAO, IITA, NAERLS and recognised agricultural institutions.

Maintain research sources, publication dates and validation records.

### MODULE 5: Soil Intelligence
Integrate soil moisture, temperature, pH, electrical conductivity and supported nutrient sensors.

Monitor soil conditions, historical readings, sensor calibration and crop-specific moisture requirements.

### MODULE 6: Weather Intelligence
Integrate weather APIs and supported agricultural environmental data services.

Monitor temperature, rainfall, humidity, wind speed, solar radiation and evapotranspiration.

Use weather information when calculating irrigation and chemical application suitability.

### MODULE 7: Intelligent Irrigation Scheduling
Implement scientifically grounded irrigation calculations using FAO evapotranspiration methodology.

Consider crop coefficients, soil-water balance, crop growth stage, effective rainfall, root-zone water availability and irrigation efficiency.

Calculate appropriate irrigation quantities, schedules and durations for each agricultural zone.

### MODULE 8: Smart Sprinkler Distribution Management
Register and control individual sprinkler devices, pumps, valves and irrigation zones.

Support scheduled, automatic, semi-automatic and manual operations.

Monitor pressure, flow rate, operating duration, water distribution and device health.

### MODULE 9: IoT Hardware Integration
Integrate ESP32, Raspberry Pi, soil sensors, weather stations, water meters, pumps, valves, cameras and other supported devices.

Use MQTT, REST APIs and WebSockets where appropriate.

Support secure device authentication, telemetry, command acknowledgement, offline operation and reconnection.

### MODULE 10: AI Agricultural Advisory
Develop an AI-powered agricultural assistant capable of answering crop-related questions, interpreting agricultural information and providing irrigation and crop management recommendations.

Ground recommendations in verified agricultural knowledge and farm-specific observations.

### MODULE 11: Water Resource Management
Manage reservoirs, boreholes, tanks, water sources, available water capacity, water consumption and water distribution.

Prevent irrigation operations that exceed configured water availability.

### MODULE 12: Crop Health and Growth Monitoring
Track planting activities, crop growth, plant health, visible disease symptoms, growth anomalies and crop development history.

Support image uploads and periodic crop assessments.

### MODULE 13: Automation and Control Centre
Develop a central automation engine supporting irrigation rules, crop protection workflows, device control, emergency shutdown, manual overrides and operational restrictions.

All physical operations must pass through safety validation.

### MODULE 14: Notification and Alert Management
Implement in-app notifications, email, SMS, WhatsApp integration and mobile push notifications.

Support scheduled, immediate, critical and escalation notifications.

### MODULE 15: Agricultural Reports and Analytics
Generate irrigation, water consumption, crop health, weed management, chemical application, security incident and harvest reports.

Support PDF and Excel exports.

### MODULE 16: Mobile Application
Develop a responsive mobile application supporting farm monitoring, crop identification, irrigation control, weed detection, security alerts, harvest reminders and agricultural advisory.

Support offline data collection and synchronisation.

### MODULE 17: Administration and Configuration
Implement comprehensive system administration, user management, crop database management, device management, agricultural parameters, permissions, API configurations, system settings, backups and audit logs.

### MODULE 18: AI Learning and Predictive Intelligence
Develop predictive models for irrigation requirements, water consumption, crop stress, crop performance and agricultural optimisation.

Use historical farm information and validated agricultural observations.

Evaluate model accuracy and prevent unvalidated predictions from independently controlling physical equipment.

### MODULE 19: AI Weed Identification and Critical Growth Detection

Develop an intelligent weed detection and management module.

Requirements:

1. Identify common and invasive weed species.
2. Distinguish weeds from cultivated crops.
3. Detect weed seedlings and growth stages.
4. Identify flowering and seed production.
5. Estimate weed density and field coverage.
6. Identify proximity to cultivated crops.
7. Assess potential competition for nutrients, water and sunlight.
8. Classify infestation as Low, Moderate, High or Critical.
9. Identify agricultural zones requiring intervention.
10. Recommend appropriate weed management strategies.
11. Record weed detection history.
12. Monitor treatment effectiveness.
13. Generate weed infestation reports.

Support camera-based identification, uploaded photographs, drone imagery where available and manual field assessments.

Use confidence scoring and human verification for uncertain identifications.

### MODULE 20: Chemical Sprinkler and Agricultural Application Management

Develop a dedicated chemical application management system.

Manage approved herbicides, pesticides, insecticides, fungicides and other authorised agricultural treatments.

Requirements:

1. Agricultural chemical inventory.
2. Chemical registration and approval records.
3. Active ingredient information.
4. Crop compatibility.
5. Target weed, pest or disease identification.
6. Product label and safety data storage.
7. Application rate management.
8. Chemical tank monitoring.
9. Spraying equipment registration.
10. Dedicated chemical spraying controllers.
11. Zone-specific application management.
12. Application scheduling.
13. Flow and volume monitoring.
14. Weather and wind restrictions.
15. Chemical leakage detection.
16. Operator authorisation.
17. Application history.
18. Equipment cleaning records.
19. Pre-harvest interval tracking.
20. Re-entry interval notifications.

Chemical spraying equipment must be independently controlled from normal irrigation equipment.

The application engine must validate the product label, target crop, approved use, application rate, operator authorisation and environmental restrictions.

AI may recommend treatment, but an authorised human operator must approve each chemical application before execution.

Never allow automatic chemical application solely on the basis of AI image identification.

### MODULE 21: AI Farm Intrusion Detection and Security Management

Develop a comprehensive farm surveillance and security notification system.

Requirements:

1. Human movement detection.

3. Wildlife detection.
4. Vehicle detection.
5. Suspicious movement identification.
6. Restricted-zone monitoring.
7. Farm entrance monitoring.
8. Night surveillance.
9. Real-time security alerts.
10. Image and video evidence recording.
11. GPS-based incident location.

### MODULE 22: AI Crop Maturity and Harvest Period Prediction

Develop an intelligent crop lifecycle and harvest forecasting system.

Every planting batch must have an independent lifecycle record.

Required information:

- Farm identification.
- Agricultural zone.
- Crop species.
- Crop variety.
- Planting date.
- Expected maturity duration.
- Growth stage.
- Estimated maturity date.
- Expected harvest window.
- Actual harvest date.
- Harvest quantity.

The system must calculate actual calendar dates using planting dates and validated crop maturity information.

Support growing degree day calculations where appropriate, alongside crop variety, local weather, crop development and field observations.

Implement:

1. Expected maturity date.
2. Expected harvest window.
3. Harvest readiness assessment.
4. Crop maturity monitoring.
5. Harvest calendar.
6. Harvest labour reminders.
7. Storage preparation reminders.
8. Transportation preparation reminders.
9. Harvest records.
10. Actual versus predicted maturity analysis.
11. Crop yield records.
12. Multiple planting cycles.

ABSOLUTE DATE NOTIFICATION REQUIREMENT:

The system must provide specific calendar dates and clock times for harvest-related notifications.

Do not use vague messages such as "harvest is approaching."

For example:

Planting Date: 01 October 2026

Expected Maturity Duration: 100 Days

Predicted Maturity Date: 09 January 2027

Notification Schedule:
- 10 December 2026: 30-day advance reminder.
- 25 December 2026: 15-day advance reminder.
- 02 January 2027: 7-day advance reminder.
- 07 January 2027: 2-day advance reminder.
- 09 January 2027: Expected maturity notification.

The dates above are illustrative.

Actual dates must be calculated from the recorded planting date and validated crop maturity parameters.

Support configurable notification intervals and local farm timezones.

Distinguish between predicted maturity and confirmed harvest readiness.

If the crop has not been harvested after the predicted date, continue reassessment and issue appropriate follow-up notifications.
